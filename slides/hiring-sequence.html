Three Gates Drive the Sequence

Hire to unlock the next milestone

① Approvals Gate

Months 0–8

  • Title DD, plan sanction, RERA registration
  • BBMP/BDA, BESCOM, BWSSB, fire, environment NOCs
  • Blocker if empty: cannot legally start or sell

② Launch Gate

Months 4–10

  • Brand, renders, funnel, CP network, EOI program
  • Sales team trained; CRM live; pricing ladder locked
  • Blocker if empty: no demand engine = no bookings = no cash

③ Mobilization Gate

Months 7–12

  • Tendering, BOQ, schedule, QA/safety setup
  • Site establishment, contractor mobilization
  • Blocker if empty: bookings made, build can't start on time

Rule — hire each gate's owner with lead time, not on the gate date: liaisoning needs ~6 months of runway, the two S&M heads need 4–5 months of pre-launch build, the Projects head needs ~2 months of tendering before the first slab.

The Pre-Launch Playbook

"A launch is won or lost in the 120 days before it. Here is exactly how I intend to run those 120 days."

— written from the MD's chair

My Non-Negotiables

Operating principles · MD's voice

01 · Compliance before commerce

Not one rupee is collected — EOI included — before RERA registration is live. One violation can freeze the entire project.

02 · Channel partners are the engine

CPs deliver ≥60% of our bookings, so they are treated as customers: payouts in 30 days flat, no disputes, no excuses. Word of a slow-paying developer travels faster than any ad campaign.

03 · Twin engines, one number

Traditional and Digital share a single booking target. I review one funnel, in one meeting, every Monday at 9 am. The day they start optimizing for credit instead of bookings, the structure has failed.

04 · Pricing discipline

The price ladder is locked before EOI opens. Discounts beyond the approved matrix come to me personally — margin leaks happen one "small exception" at a time.

05 · Sell what the renders promise

3D renders, the experience center and the legal spec sheet must agree. Over-promising in launch creative is borrowing from delivery-day reputation.

06 · No launch without readiness

If the team isn't trained, the CRM isn't routing, or CP momentum is weak — the launch moves. A delayed launch costs weeks; a failed launch costs the project's pricing power.

EOI & Pricing Strategy

Momentum is manufactured before launch day

EOI Mechanics

  • Refundable priority pass: ₹50K–2L by ticket size
  • Benefits: allotment priority, launch-day price advantage (₹100–150/sqft), unit-choice order
  • Collected only post-RERA, fully documented
  • Target: 1.5–2× EOIs vs. launch-day inventory released

Price Ladder

  • EOI holders: best rate (the reward for early trust)
  • Launch price: EOI + ₹100–150/sqft
  • Escalation: +3–5% per ~25% inventory absorbed
  • Discount matrix approved once; exceptions only via MD

Inventory Release

  • Release 40–50% at launch — scarcity is real, not theatrical
  • Hold premium stock (corners, high floors, garden-facing) for later phases
  • Tower-wise release synced to construction milestones & cashflow

"The EOI book is my launch insurance. If EOIs are weak at T-30, I'd rather move the launch than burn the price ladder on day one. Velocity can be bought; pricing power, once lost, cannot."

Sequencing Risks & Mitigations

What breaks the plan

Notice-Period Lag

Senior hires carry 60–90-day notices. Mitigation: offers land a quarter before in-seat dates; buy out notice for the critical path.

Approvals Slip

Gate 1 slip cascades into launch & mobilization. Mitigation: Hire #1 is the day-zero seat; over-resource consultants behind them.

Twin-Head Friction

Two S&M heads can drift into credit wars. Mitigation: one shared booking number, one funnel review, CP attribution rules in CRM from day one.

CP Network Cold-Start

CPs produce for developers they trust. Mitigation: hire #2 for their rolodex; 30-day payout SLA honored from the first cheque.

Digital CPL Inflation

Bangalore RE auction costs keep rising. Mitigation: portal + CP + referral mix; CPL red line triggers channel rebalance, not budget creep.

Engineer Crunch at Mobilization

Site engineers are scarce when many projects mobilize. Mitigation: Senior Engineer hired M9 builds the bench; contractor-deputed staff as a bridge.

Cash discipline — seats 7–12 are deliberately hire-on-demand so payroll scales with the project. Only the four gate owners + finance pair are committed up front.
