Three Gates Drive the Sequence

Hire to unlock the next milestone — not by org-chart tidiness

① Approvals Gate

Months 0–8

  • Land due diligence, plan sanction, RERA registration
  • BBMP/BDA, BESCOM, BWSSB, fire, environment NOCs
  • Blocker if empty: project cannot legally start or sell

② Launch Gate

Months 6–10

  • Brand, pricing, channel-partner network, pre-launch funnel
  • Sales team trained, CRM & collateral live
  • Blocker if empty: no demand engine = no bookings = no cashflow

③ Mobilization Gate

Months 9–13

  • Contractor tendering, BOQ, schedule, QA/safety setup
  • Site establishment, vendor mobilization
  • Blocker if empty: bookings made, but build can't start on time

Rule of thumb — hire each milestone's owner with lead time, not on the milestone date. A liaisoning hire needs ~6 months runway; an S&M head needs ~3–4 months of pre-launch; a Projects head needs ~2 months for tendering before the first slab.

Hire #1 — JD · Liaisoning & Approvals Manager

Reports to MD · In seat M0

Mission: Secure every statutory approval and NOC required to legally launch and build the project, on schedule and without surprises — converting land into a sanctioned, RERA-registered, sellable asset.

Key Outcomes (12 mo)

  • Plan sanction & commencement certificate secured by agreed date
  • RERA registration live before any sale/advertisement
  • All utility NOCs (BESCOM, BWSSB, fire, environment) cleared
  • Zero stop-work / compounding notices in year one
  • Approvals tracker & cost map maintained, no missed renewals

Responsibilities

  • Drive BBMP/BDA, RERA, KSPCB, fire & utility submissions
  • Manage architects, RCC & legal consultants, liaisoning agents
  • Title due diligence, khata, conversion, encumbrance checks
  • Government & ward-level relationship management
  • Approvals budget, timelines & risk reporting to MD

Must-Have

  • 8–14 yrs liaisoning for residential/mixed-use in Bangalore
  • Hands-on BBMP/BDA/RERA & utility track record
  • Existing govt & consultant network in the relevant zones
  • Strong on Karnataka real-estate law & documentation

Nice-to-Have

  • Environmental clearance / high-rise experience
  • Civil / law / planning background
  • Experience with a comparable mid-size developer
  • Kannada fluency for ward-level work

Hire #2 — JD · Head of Sales & Marketing

Reports to MD · In seat M5

Mission: Build the demand engine — brand, pricing, channel network and a trained sales team — so the project hits its pre-launch and launch booking targets and converts inventory to cashflow.

Key Outcomes (launch +90d)

  • Pre-launch EOI / booking target achieved before public launch
  • Channel-partner network (CPs) onboarded & producing
  • Cost-per-qualified-lead and conversion within target
  • Sales team hired, trained, scripted; CRM live
  • Pricing & inventory-release strategy signed off with MD

Responsibilities

  • Own GTM: brand, positioning, pricing, launch plan
  • Build & manage CP + digital + walk-in demand channels
  • Recruit and lead sales execs; set targets & incentives
  • Marketing budget, agency & collateral management
  • Weekly funnel, booking & collection reporting to MD

Must-Have

  • 10–16 yrs residential real-estate S&M, Bangalore market
  • Has launched & sold out at least one project / tower
  • Strong CP network + digital demand-gen literacy
  • Built and led sales teams to target

Nice-to-Have

  • Branded-developer pedigree (Prestige/Sobha/Brigade tier)
  • CRM (Sell.Do / Salesforce) & performance-marketing depth
  • NRI / luxury segment experience
  • MBA marketing

Hire #3 — JD · Projects / Construction Head

Reports to MD · In seat M7

Mission: Take the sanctioned project from paper to mobilized site — tendering, BOQ, schedule, quality & safety — and deliver to spec, on budget, on the committed RERA date.

Key Outcomes (mobilize +120d)

  • Contractors tendered & awarded; BOQ & rates locked
  • Master schedule baselined; site established & mobilized
  • QA/QC & safety (EHS) systems operational from day one
  • Cost & schedule variance held within tolerance
  • RERA quarterly progress commitments met

Responsibilities

  • Tendering, vendor selection, contracts & rate negotiation
  • Planning, BOQ, billing certification & cost control
  • Site execution, quality, safety & statutory site compliance
  • Manage site engineers, contractors & consultants
  • Progress, cost & risk reporting to MD

Must-Have

  • 12–18 yrs civil/project execution on residential builds
  • Delivered ≥1 G+ high-rise / comparable project end-to-end
  • Strong on tendering, BOQ, billing & cost control
  • Bangalore contractor & vendor network

Nice-to-Have

  • RERA-project delivery & QPR experience
  • MEP coordination & modern construction tech (Mivan/precast)
  • PMP / planning-software (Primavera/MSP) fluency
  • B.E Civil + M.Tech / construction mgmt

Sequencing Risks & Mitigations

What breaks the gate plan

Notice-Period Lag

Senior real-estate hires carry 60–90 day notices. Mitigation: start sourcing Hire #1 today; buy out / negotiate early joins for critical path.

Approvals Slip

If Gate 1 slips, Launch & Mobilize cascade. Mitigation: Hire #1 is the single non-negotiable seat; over-resource consultants behind them.

S&M Head Too Late

Hiring at launch = no pre-launch funnel. Mitigation: in seat M5, a full ~4 months before public launch.

Comp Drift

Branded-developer counter-offers. Mitigation: benchmark live, lead with variable upside & ownership scope, decide fast.

Cash discipline — seats 5–8 are deliberately "hire on demand" so payroll scales with the project, not ahead of it. Only the three gate-owners + Finance are committed up front.
